THE CHALLENGE

Why water is scarce
in Turkana.

Turkana is hot and very dry, with low and unreliable rainfall. Drought can leave rivers and other surface sources dry, but climate is only part of the problem: reaching and maintaining a reliable water supply can also be difficult.

01

Little rain, high heat. Rainfall is scarce and unpredictable, and heat quickly evaporates water from soil and open sources.

02

Few dependable sources. Many households and pastoral communities rely on groundwater, which can be deep, costly to pump, or naturally salty.

03

Access is not guaranteed. Long distances, limited infrastructure, high water costs, and pump breakdowns can interrupt supply even where a source exists.

THE SOLUTIONS

Practical ways to
make every drop count.

In Turkana’s dry climate, where communities may be far from reliable water sources, solutions need to fit local conditions and daily needs. Protecting and improving water points, using suitable pumps, and storing water can help households and livestock through dry periods. Communities should help guide what is built and how it is maintained.

01 — THE RESOURCE WE SHARE

Bring clean water closer

In Turkana, maintaining boreholes and water points and checking water quality can help people access safer water closer to home.

02 — A SYSTEM IN MOTION

Use wind to move water

Where wind and groundwater conditions are suitable, wind-powered pumps can help supply water for Turkana homes, livestock and small-scale farming. Equipment needs local maintenance.

03 — EVERY DROP COUNTS

Save water for dry days

In Turkana, tanks can store rainwater when it falls or hold water delivered from a source, helping households manage supplies between replenishment.
